INFRA SENSE AI - INSTITUTIONAL SECTOR OPERATIONAL REFERENCE MANUAL
========================================================================
Tagline: "Efficiency. Sustainability. Comfort."
Scope: Smart University Campus HVAC Zoning & Server Cooling Logs

This document serves as the primary technical reference for the Institutional Sector 
monitoring system integrated within the InfraSense AI platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GENERAL INFRASTRUCTURE & VITAL METRICS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system monitors academic servers, HVAC zoning, and campus carbon footprints 
across 8 primary telemetry metrics:
  * Telemetry Health: Operational status of hardware sensors (%).
  * Chill Water Cooling Flow: Water velocity index inside coolant lines (kJ).
  * Server Room Temperature: Indoor climate temperature averages (°C).
  * Airflow Velocity: Active ventilation intake pressure indexes (m/s).
  * Main Substation Voltage: Campus power station delivery inputs (V).
  * HVAC Efficiency: Running thermal performance ratios.
  * Active Systems Count: Active campus HVAC zones currently monitored.
  * AI Core Risk Score: Probability of HVAC zone system cooling failure (%).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HARDWARE DEVICES MONITORED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * HVAC Climate Intake Fan:
     * Model: Carrier 39M Series Main Intake.
     * Voltage Range: 208V - 230V (Fluctuates dynamically around 228.4V).
     * Optimal Threshold: Warns if voltage deviates below 208V or exceeds 230V.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MACHINE LEARNING MODELLING PIPELINE (university.py)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Dataset Ingest: "university.csv"
  * Primary Predictors: server_load_kw, chilled_water_kj, outdoor_temp_c, humidity_pct, hvac_runtime_hr.
  * Client-Side Model Replica:
    Computes real-time failure probabilities and zone risks based on thermal index drift.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. MANUAL SIMULATION OVERRIDES (CRASH TESTS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
To validate critical alert HUD logic, three manual overrides are available in the UI:
  A. Emergency Monitor Test (CRIMSON EMERGENCY MODE):
     * Simulates server room chilled water cooling flow collapsing to zero.
     * Alarms turn red; prompts operator to initiate immediate workloads migration to cloud.
  B. Degradation Tracker Test (AMBER WARNING MODE):
     * Simulates long hvac runtimes and compressor efficiency decay.
     * Alarms turn amber; prompts operator to log a preventive compressor maintenance ticket.
  C. Environment Optimizer Test (TEAL OPTIMIZATION MODE):
     * Simulates high outdoor heat index and humidity instability.
     * Alarms turn teal; prompts operator to execute smart cooling balance scripts.
