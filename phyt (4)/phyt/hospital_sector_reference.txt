INFRA SENSE AI - SMART HOSPITAL MONITORING & ANOMALY DETECTION SYSTEM
========================================================================
Tagline: "Predict. Detect. Prevent."
Operational Scope: Hospital Sector Dashboard & Health AI Core

This reference document outlines the key components, parameters, and AI model 
architectures integrated into the Hospital Sector operational domain.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GENERAL INFRASTRUCTURE & VITAL METRICS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system processes continuous patient telemetry and clinical loads across 8 
primary glowing KPI monitors:
  * Deterioration Score: Continuous risk assessment (0-100 scale).
  * ICU Admission Probability: Likelihood of escalating care (0-100%).
  * Emergency Readmission Risk: 30-day readmission prediction (0-100%).
  * Doctor/Staff Workload: Active patient load per clinical practitioner.
  * Critical Bed Occupancy: ICU and emergency ward space saturation (%).
  * Combined Risk Index: Integrated vital-sign distress indicator.
  * Active Hospital Anomalies: Unacknowledged clinical warning states.
  * Clinical Core Health: Operational infrastructure status (%)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. AI RANDOM FOREST MODEL REPLICAS (CLIENT-SIDE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The client-side prediction engine runs deterministic replicas of the trained 
Random Forest models based on "hospital.csv" telemetry:

  A. Classifier Model Replica (predictTrainedModel):
     * Input Features: Patient age, stay days, medications, workload, bed occupancy.
     * Categorical Isolation: 
       - "Normal Operations" (Nominal biosignals)
       - "Silent Patient Deterioration" (Elevated deterioration, ICU history)
       - "Recovery Degradation Pattern" (Low recovery status, high stay duration)
       - "Hospital Resource Overload" (90%+ bed occupancy, heavy doctor load)

  B. Regressor Model Replica (ICU Escalation/Risk):
     * Computes the continuous ICU risk index using a weighted linear representation:
       Risk = (StayDays * 1.5) + (Meds * 2.2) + (Workload * 0.8) + (Occupancy * 0.6)
     * R-squared (R2): 79.02% precision target.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DYNAMIC SIMULATION OVERRIDES (CRASH TESTS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
To validate medical alert logic and neon telemetry transitions, three 
specialized manual crash testing modules are built into the UI:

  A. Silent Patient Deterioration (CRIMSON EMERGENCY MODE):
     * Action: Simulates critical cardiovascular/respiratory collapse.
     * Dashboard Response: Cards flash Crimson; triggers automated ICU 
       escalation prompts and rapid response notifications.

  B. Recovery Decay Pattern (AMBER WARNING MODE):
     * Action: Simulates gradual patient recovery decay after surgery.
     * Dashboard Response: Shift to Amber glow; triggers post-op medication 
       audit and dynamic discharge postponement recommendations.

  C. Hospital Resource Saturation (TEAL OPTIMIZATION MODE):
     * Action: Simulates a mass casualty or critical ward overload.
     * Dashboard Response: Shift to Teal accents; triggers dynamic bed 
       reallocation and automated staff scheduling adjustment logs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. 10 INTERACTIVE RECHARTS VISUALIZATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ten specialized chart widgets are mapped to track clinical workflows:
  1. Real-time Patient Vitals: Compares Deterioration vs Combined Risk scores.
  2. ICU Risk Trend Graph: Multi-hour risk timeline monitoring.
  3. Recovery Degradation Timeline: Dotted forecast showing predicted decay.
  4. Readmission Prediction Model: Dashed confidence intervals for discharge.
  5. Resource Utilization Grid: Occupancy vs workload matrix.
  6. Hospital Risk Gauge: Radial Threat Assessment Dial.
  7. Bed Occupancy Analytics: Shaded area chart tracking capacity.
  8. Doctor Workload Graph: Comparison bars tracking clinician overload.
  9. Anomaly Distribution: Pie chart breaking down active anomaly types.
  10. Treatment Complexity: Stacked bar chart mapping complex care metrics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. LOCALIZED CLINICAL AI ASSISTANT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Isolated floating widget in the bottom right (and dedicated console tab).
* Uses the separate "hospitalChatMessages" state to avoid sharing data 
  with the Residential sector.
* Translates between English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) on the fly.
* Provides root-cause diagnostics for medical telemetry queries (e.g., ICU 
  risk explanation, resource load balancing, and readmission reviews).
