1. GENERAL INFRASTRUCTURE & 8 DYNAMIC KPI METRICS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system monitors household grids and substation feeds across 8 primary KPI indicators:
  1. Residential Consumption: Rolling total drawing load (e.g. 194 kWh baseline).
  2. Active Anomalies: Flagged smart grid anomalies and bypasses (e.g. 2 Critical Alerts baseline).
  3. Critical Grid Alerts: Immediate inspection required flags from high-risk nodes.
  4. Electrical Health: Aggregate line factor efficiency score (%).
  5. Predicted Risk Level: Random Forest classifier likelihood prediction (e.g. 82% Risk).
  6. Peak Usage Window: Daily highest load cycle averages (18:00 - 21:00).
  7. Monthly Energy Cost: Simulated running billing cost based on current draw ($).
  8. AI Core Accuracy: Trained validation accuracy fit score (95.8% ACC).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATASET SCHEMAS & MODEL FEATURE COLUMNS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system integrates with trained Random Forest and LSTM prediction models based on 13 features:
  * apartment ID (Apartment identification tag)
  * timestamp (Vitals recording interval)
  * voltage (Current grid line voltage in Volts)
  * current (Substation current flow in Amperes)
  * power consumption (Load demand in kW)
  * energy usage (kWh) (Electrical consumption in kilowatt-hours)
  * frequency (Ingestion frequency in Hz)
  * power factor (Power factor coefficient optimal targets)
  * appliance usage (Smart mechanical draw signatures)
  * weather temperature (Ambient outdoor temperature in °C)
  * peak load (High daily loading cycle indicators)
  * night usage (Suspicious midnight activity markers)
  * anomaly labels (Warning classification statuses)

4. 6 ANOMALIES TO DETECT & DIAGNOSTIC PROFILES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A. Power Theft (Critical Severity - Red):
     - Suspicious meter bypasses, abnormal drops, radial SVG theft probability gauge (82%).
     - AI Insight: "Possible unauthorized electricity usage detected in Apartment APT_1027."
     - Recommendation: "Dispatch Field Meter Integrity Inspection Team"

  B. Unexpected Spike (Warning Severity - Orange):
     - Sudden electricity surges, line spikes graph with highlighted red anomaly markers.
     - AI Insight: "Electricity usage increased abnormally within short duration."
     - Recommendation: "Reduce High-Power Appliance Usage"

  C. Appliance Fault (Warning Severity - Orange):
     - Mechanical wear signature alerts, appliance health progress bars, fault probability.
     - AI Recommendation: "Air Conditioner may require maintenance."

  D. Continuous Overload (High Severity - Orange):
     - Long-duration heavy load monitoring, transformer thermal stress gauge, infrastructure risk.
     - AI Alert: "Continuous overload may damage infrastructure."
     - Recommendation: "Engage Transformer Phase-Shifter"

  E. Abnormal Midnight Usage (Low Severity - Yellow):
     - Night occupancy mismatch comparison (0% occupancy vs 4.2 kW load), midnight timelines.
     - AI Insight: "Unusual energy consumption detected during midnight hours."
     - Recommendation: "Trigger Autonomous Load Mitigation"

  F. Weather Mismatch Usage (Medium Severity - Yellow):
     - Mapped external cool temperature vs high indoor HVAC draw (low correlation fit < 0.12).
     - AI Insight: "Current energy usage does not match weather conditions."
     - Recommendation: "Activate Smart Energy Optimization"
