INFRA SENSE AI - INDUSTRIAL SECTOR OPERATIONAL REFERENCE MANUAL
========================================================================
Tagline: "Precision. Safety. Continuity."
Scope: Heavy Duty Manufacturing & Rotational Telemetry Systems

This document serves as the primary technical reference for the Industrial Sector 
monitoring system integrated within the InfraSense AI platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GENERAL INFRASTRUCTURE & VITAL METRICS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system monitors heavy-duty assembly automation machinery, conveyor motors, and 
pneumatic air pressure logs across 8 primary telemetry metrics:
  * Telemetry Health: Operational status of hardware sensors (%).
  * Rotation Vibration: Rotational motor shaft vibration (mm/s).
  * Compressed Air Flow: Air velocity index within pneumatic lines (m/s).
  * Hydraulic Temperature: Running core motor temperatures (°C).
  * Operational Voltage: Electrical voltage input to main systems (V).
  * Active Systems Count: Monitored assembly lines currently online.
  * Detected Anomalies: Active alert flags in the industrial sector.
  * AI Core Risk Score: Probability of preventative mechanical fatigue failure (%).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HARDWARE DEVICES MONITORED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A. Heavy Duty Conveyor Motor:
     * Model: Siemens 1LA9 Three-Phase Core.
     * Voltage Range: 380V - 415V (Fluctuates dynamically around 412.5V).
     * Optimal Threshold: Warns if voltage deviates below 380V or exceeds 415V.

  B. Air Compressor Motor:
     * Model: Atlas Copco GA37 Pneumatic.
     * Voltage Range: 400V - 480V (Fluctuates dynamically around 415.8V).
     * Optimal Threshold: Warns if voltage approaches upper or lower limits.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CHART VISUALIZATIONS & INSTRUMENTATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Sensory Telemetry Log: LineChart comparing temperature and line voltage fluctuations.
* Operational Fatigue: PieChart tracking rotational acoustic noise profiles.
* Airflow Velocity Logs: Shaded AreaChart monitoring pneumatic lines pressure draws.
* Anomaly Flag Ingestion: BarChart tracking historical mechanical anomaly flag indices.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. AUTOMATED PREVENTATIVE ACTIONS (DISPATCH LOGIC)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When anomalies are detected, the system provides three action dispatches:
  * Perform Bearing Lubrication: Issued when rotational vibrations drift upwards.
  * Initiate Pressure Seal Calibration: Issued when compressed airflow pressure leaks.
  * Deploy Core Cooling Maintenance: Issued when motor operating temperatures overheat.
