1. GENERAL INFRASTRUCTURE & VITAL METRICS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system monitors critical care department vitals, patient monitors, and clinician 
workloads across 8 primary telemetry metrics:
  * Patient Deterioration: Continuous cardiovascular drift (0-100 score).
  * ICU Escalation Probability: Likelihood of escalating ward status (0-100%).
  * Emergency Readmission Risk: 30-day post-op readmission prediction (0-100%).
  * Doctor/Staff Workload: Active patient load per clinician index.
  * Critical Bed Occupancy: Emergency and ICU ward space capacity (%).
  * Combined Risk Score: Integrated vital sign distress indicator.
  * Active Clinical Anomalies: Unacknowledged alert warning flags.
  * Core Telemetry Stability: Operational clinical network core health (%).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HARDWARE DEVICES MONITORED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A. ICU Ventilator Backup Supply:
     * Model: Puritan Bennett 980.
     * Voltage Range: 12V - 24V DC (Fluctuates dynamically around 23.8V).
     * Optimal Threshold: Warns if voltage deviates below 12V or exceeds 24V.

  B. ICU Patient Monitor Battery:
     * Model: Philips Intellivue MX800.
     * Voltage Range: 15V - 19V DC (Fluctuates dynamically around 18.2V).
     * Optimal Threshold: Warns if voltage approaches battery safety limits.

  C. Emergency Backup Generator:
     * Model: Caterpillar C15 Diesel Core.
     * Voltage Range: 400V - 480V (Fluctuates dynamically around 478.5V).
     * Optimal Threshold: Warns if power output drifts unstable.

3. SUB-NAVIGATION SYSTEM (10 CONTEXT VIEWS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Clinical Dashboard: Dynamic glowing widgets, alert feeds, and vitals.
  2. Telemetry Flow: Floating vitals lines, ventilator logs, and core outputs.
  3. Anomaly Center: Dedicated alerts focusing on Silent Patient Deterioration.
  4. ICU Risk Analyzer: Escalation risk regressions and hourly risk timelines.
  5. Wards Capacity: Map of Active Beds (ICU, Emergency, Post-Op) and occupancy.
  6. AI Inference Panel: Deep classification HUD, logs, action dispatches.
  7. Patient Registry: bed logs, chronic histories, clinical status pills.
  8. Analytics Reports: Simulated Excel/PDF diagnostic document exporters.
  9. Trilingual Assistant: Dedicated console mapping clinical health queries.
  10. Systems Settings: Patient notification threshold selectors.

4. MACHINE LEARNING MODELLING PIPELINE (hospital.py)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Dataset Ingest: "hospital.csv"
  * Classifier (hospital_classifier.joblib):
    - Goal: Isolate clinical alert classes (Silent Patient Deterioration, etc.)
    - Accuracy: 80.00%
  * Regressor (hospital_regressor.joblib / risk_regressor.joblib):
    - Goal: Forecasts patient physical deterioration scale scores.
    - MAE: 9.47 points | R-squared (R2): 79.02%
  * Top-5 Sensory Predictive Features:
    1. previous_admissions (23.06%)
    2. hospital_stay_days (18.18%)
    3. doctor_workload (10.87%)
    4. combined_risk_score (10.75%)
    5. bed_occupancy_percent (7.96%)
